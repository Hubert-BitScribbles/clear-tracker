Clear Tracker

Privacy

Last updated 9 October 2026

In short

Clear Tracker doesn't collect anything about you. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you log stays on your device and never leaves it unless you take it somewhere yourself.

What you log stays on your device

The days you log, your intentions and your settings are stored in your browser's storage on the device you're using. They aren't sent to bitScribbles or anyone else. bitScribbles can't see your record, and can't recover it if it's lost, so back up from time to time.

Backups

A backup is a file you save yourself, wherever you choose. It's encrypted with a passphrase you choose, and without that passphrase nobody — bitScribbles included — can read it.

Loading the app

Clear Tracker is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. As with any website, opening it means your browser asks Cloudflare's servers for the app's files, which involves your IP address and browser details. Cloudflare handles that under its own privacy policy. Those requests carry nothing you've logged. Clear Tracker sets no cookies, and after the first visit it works offline.

If you contact us

Feedback and problem reports are emails you send from your own mail app, to support@bitscribbles.com. We receive what you send: your email address and your message. A problem report adds technical details — the app version, browser, device, screen and a few settings — which you can read before sending. It never includes your record. We use emails only to reply and to fix problems, never share or sell them, and will delete yours if you ask.

Please don't include health or personal details beyond how to reach you.

Links to other sites

The app links to help lines, programmes and a page to support bitScribbles. Those sites have their own privacy policies; nothing about you goes with the link.

Changes

If this changes, this page will say so, with a new date at the top. Questions: support@bitscribbles.com.
