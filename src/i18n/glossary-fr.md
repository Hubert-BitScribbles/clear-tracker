# French glossary

The words Clear Tracker uses for its ideas in French, so every screen says the
same thing the same way. Proposed 17 October 2026, for review by a French
speaker in France; Quebec's differences are in `fr-CA.json`.

**Address:** vous throughout (Hugh, 17 October 2026), as French and Quebec
health services and help lines do. Plain and kind, never clinical.

**Typography:** a non-breaking space before « : », « ? », « ; » and inside
« guillemets »; « 63 % »; times as « 8 h – 23 h ».

**Dates and numbers** come from the browser: « 17 octobre 2026 », « 1 234 »,
« 12 € » (France) and « 12 $ » (Canada). Months and weekdays are lowercase in
French, so sentences avoid starting with one.

**Quebec (`fr-CA.json`):** « fin de semaine » for week-end, « courriel » for
e-mail, « signets » for favoris. Only lines that differ go there.

**To check on a phone:** menu names in install and reminder steps (Safari,
Chrome, Samsung Internet, Google Agenda, Rappels).
